---
question: How many 250 ml glasses could you fill from a one-cubic-metre water tank?
answer: 4000
unit: glasses
assumptions:
  - One cubic metre contains 1,000 litres.
  - Every glass is filled to exactly 250 ml with no spillage.
---
The tank holds 1,000 litres, which is 1,000,000 ml. Divide by 250 ml per glass to get 4,000 glasses.
