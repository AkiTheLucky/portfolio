---
question: How many times does a human heart beat in an 80-year life?
answer: 2900000000
unit: beats
assumptions:
  - A resting heart beats about 70 times per minute.
  - The average holds roughly steady across a lifetime.
  - The person lives to 80.
---
At 70 beats per minute, a heart beats about 100,000 times a day. Over 80 years that is close to 2.9 billion beats.
