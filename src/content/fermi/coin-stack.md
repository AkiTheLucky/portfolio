---
question: How many 2 mm coins would you need to stack to reach the height of a 10-storey building?
answer: 15000
unit: coins
assumptions:
  - A storey is approximately 3 metres high.
  - Every coin is 2 mm thick and the stack has no gaps.
---
A 10-storey building is about 30 metres, or 30,000 mm. Divide 30,000 by 2 mm per coin to get approximately 15,000 coins.
