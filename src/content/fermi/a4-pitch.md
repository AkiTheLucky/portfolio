---
question: How many A4 sheets would cover a football pitch measuring 105 × 68 metres if you laid them flat without overlap?
answer: 114500
unit: sheets
assumptions:
  - An A4 sheet measures 210 × 297 mm.
  - The pitch is a perfect rectangle and partial sheets count as whole sheets.
---
The pitch covers 7,140 m². An A4 sheet covers about 0.06237 m². Dividing the two gives roughly 114,500 sheets.
