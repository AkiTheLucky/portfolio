---
question: How much does the air in a typical living room weigh?
answer: 60
unit: kilograms
assumptions:
  - The room is about 50 cubic metres, roughly 5 by 4 by 2.5 metres.
  - Air weighs about 1.2 kilograms per cubic metre.
---
Fifty cubic metres of air at 1.2 kilograms each comes to about 60 kilograms, roughly the weight of an adult.
