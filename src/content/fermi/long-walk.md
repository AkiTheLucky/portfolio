---
question: Approximately how many steps would a person take to walk 1,000 km with an average step length of 75 cm?
answer: 1333000
unit: steps
assumptions:
  - Every step is exactly 75 cm on average.
  - The full distance is actually walked.
---
1,000 km is 1,000,000 metres. Divide by 0.75 metres per step for approximately 1.33 million steps.
