I actually love the current AI media landscape. No one knows for certain what is happening, and everything points in all directions: ["It's the atom bomb."](https://www.theverge.com/podcast/1015408/bill-gates-ai-interview-microsoft-openai-anthropic-risk-policy-regulation) ["It's a bubble."](https://futurism.com/future-society/stock-market-bloodbath-ai-bubble-earnings) ["It's the biggest thing we have ever seen."](https://www.wired.com/story/its-time-to-believe-the-ai-hype/) ["We won't need SWEs in six months."](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-ceo-ai-90-percent-code-3-to-6-months-2025-3) ["We won't need white-collar jobs, we just need SWEs."](https://www.businessinsider.com/dario-amodei-ai-essay-most-interesting-quotes-2026-1) ["We just need project managers."](https://stackoverflow.blog/2026/08/20/ai-won-t-replace-project-managers-but-it-is-reshaping-how-work-gets-done/) ["Let's force AI use on our staff."](https://www.eweek.com/news/meta-ai-performance-reviews/) ["Let's take AI coding tools away from our staff."](https://the-decoder.com/goldman-sachs-pulls-ai-coding-tools-from-traders-and-bankers/)

That is a lot of certainty for a field where nobody agrees and everyone's answer depends on their background. I think the best thing one can do is stay calm.

Going in front of a class of students and telling them their career won't exist soon, as [an Anthropic researcher recently did](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2111873903970881738), is realistic in the sense that jobs will change over time. It is also catastrophic for young people looking to adults for security and answers. It doesn't help that his call to action was to use more of the product he sells.

The same sales pitch that fills a classroom echoes in the stock market.

I love these short-term hyperbolic views on growth too. I think people forget that we live in a very globalized world, and saturation of a market happens so insanely quickly nowadays that the people chasing it crash into the ceiling head-on when they realize it isn't an infinite money glitch.

I remember [a meme](https://x.com/trajektoriePL/status/2114605335696585174) about expectations for AI growing just as exponentially as its capabilities. The first panel has AI failing at 2+2. The second has it offering "a non-elegant solution to a millennium math problem." Both times, the person reacts with the same flat "look, AI still isn't good for anything."

But what other choice do we have? Every time we adjust our expectations, the AI labs and the stock market tell us: "Actually, it's not just a productivity tool, it's going to replace you." Then: "Actually, it's not just going to replace you, it will kill you." Like, stop promising greater and greater things. Maybe then people would finally get to what AI can and cannot do.

My last thought is that I am genuinely curious whether AGI or SI will ever be real. Can humans really create something smarter than us, able to create new things we couldn't? It is a fascinating question, and it will shock the entire world whichever way it goes. If AGI does come to be, what then? If it doesn't, will certain people go into the history books as the greatest con men ever, along with our global economy? Super curious what will happen.

Until then, I will keep grinding [Boot.dev](https://www.boot.dev/u/akilucky).

See you next time.
